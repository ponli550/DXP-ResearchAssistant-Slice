      How the desk works

      From a question to the page that proves it.

      1. Pick your reportsUse the reports button under the question box, or tick them in the side panel.
      2. Ask in plain wordsYou see each step as it runs, then an answer with its page, or a plain "no answer".
      3. Check the sourceHover a page tag to preview the quote. Select it to open the page with the quote marked.
      4. Compare editionsChoose "Table across reports" to run one question in every report and see how a figure moved.
      5. Add your own PDFUse the + button, or drop a file on the page. Only you can search it, and deleting it removes the answers that used it.
